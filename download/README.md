- `LEVERAGE_v2.5.0_XOGOT_project.zip` (123 MB) — for **Xogot on iPhone/iPad**:
  1) in the Files app tap the zip ONCE to extract (open the extracted
  FOLDER, never the zip, in Xogot), 2) open it in Xogot, 3) press Play.
  The asset cache is pre-imported so the first open is fast.
  Full steps + troubleshooting: `FIRST_READ.txt` inside the zip and
  `docs/final_verification_guide.md`.

## What is new in v2.5.0 (open the game and check these)
1. **Boot** — the animated DE YOUNG GAMES ident (badge draw-on, wordmark
   light sweep, ident sting), then the LEVERAGE title card.
2. **New game** — the opening is a conversation now: camera flights over the
   city, Rizzo and Dez talking (portraits), Tommy's letter, the threatening
   phone call. Tap X to advance a line, HOLD X to skip.
3. **The river** — drive west from the Old Quarter: bridges, marina, fish
   jumping, gulls circling.
4. **The train** — the elevated Cinder Line crosses the north of the city
   every ~95 seconds; horns at the stations.
5. **Gas stations** — Dez Depot (Docklands) and East Strip Fuel. The pumps
   are explosive — shoot one.
6. **Night** — windows light up warm, storefronts get neon (pink/cyan/
   orange/green), streetlamps pool. On-device bloom makes it glow.
7. **Difficulty** — later chapters field tougher crews (the chapter card
   names the tier: STREETS -> LEVERAGE).
8. Missions are longer arcs (Debt First is now five stages), and every
   campaign mission opens with a chapter title card.
