# LEVERAGE — Story Bible
### The first entry in the DE YOUNG GAMES universe
**Everyone has leverage on you.**

---

## 1. The DE YOUNG GAMES Universe

DE YOUNG GAMES builds **city-first crime worlds** where the city is a character,
every system feeds the story, and every ending costs something. LEVERAGE is the
first entry: a self-contained story in **Cinder City** that establishes the
universe's rules — debts are inherited, information is currency, and nobody in
this town is clean.

Future DE YOUNG GAMES titles revisit Cinder City from new angles (a getaway
driver's night, a homicide detective's ledger of unsolved cases, the
syndicate's rise decades earlier). Characters cross over. Consequences echo.
LEVERAGE is where the ledger opens.

**Studio pillars (the DE YOUNG signature):**
1. **One city, one story, no filler.** Every mission moves the Ledger plot.
2. **Choices with weight.** Four endings, none free.
3. **A living world.** Rivers with fish, trains on schedules, birds that
   scatter when guns come out — the city breathes whether you watch or not.
4. **Amber and neon.** The visual language: warm streetlight amber against
   cold neon night.

## 2. Cinder City

A port city split by the **Cinder River**. Money lives on the east bank; debt
lives on the west. The **Cinder Line** elevated train stitches the halves
together above the north of the city, and the great bridge on Main Cross
carries everything else.

| District | Soul | In play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Old Quarter** | Markets, alleys, family stores being squeezed | Dez's home, Rizzo's market, Marisol's Bar, Dez Depot filling station |
| **Docklands** | Containers, impound, the container yard | Repo jobs, Tommy's rescue, dropped stashes |
| **Kestrel Financial District** | Glass, money, cameras | Kestrel Tower and the vault heist |
| **Neon Strip** | Clubs, lights, cash flow | Sabotage job, the substation, night traffic |
| **Ironworks** | Scrap, sweat, Big Theo's gym | Lucy Fang's scrapyard war, strike-breaking |
| **The Heights** | Villas above the fog | The Castellane Villa, the finale |
| **The Cinder River** | The city's spine | Marina, gulls, fish shoals, the bridge |

**Living city systems (all real, all running):** day/night with golden-hour
light, weather and rain, scheduled train with horn and stations, pigeon flocks
that scatter at violence, gulls over the river, rats in the alleys at night,
fireflies in the green patches, moths at the streetlights, fish that jump,
explosive filling-station pumps, radio stations, phone with contacts and
messages, street encounters, wanted system 1–5 stars with air support.

## 3. The Ledger (the MacGuffin that is also the theme)

The Family keeps its real books in **the Ledger**: not money — **leverage**.
Who owes, who cheats, who talked to the police, who has a daughter in a
clinic they'd pay anything to keep quiet. Tommy Carter ran numbers for the
Family until a page of the Ledger went missing. Now the Family holds Tommy,
and Tommy's debt falls to his brother.

Every chapter Dez collects leverage of his own — and the game keeps asking:
**when you hold everyone's secrets, are you still the good one?**

## 5. Campaign structure (what ships)

**15 story missions across 5 chapters, 8 side jobs, 4 endings.** Missions are
multi-stage arcs (setup → escalation → twist → fallout) — not five-minute
errands. Difficulty ramps by chapter (the Difficulty director: +22% enemy HP
per chapter, +damage, +bodies on non-boss waves).

- **Chapter 1 — Debt First.** The market fight, the repo'd sedan, the rendezvous
  choice (give the car to the Family or keep the papers as leverage).
- **Chapter 2 — Pressure.** Big Theo's gym (kickboxing unlocked), Marisol's bar
  night against Lucy Fang (mercy / brutality / police tip).
- **Chapter 3 — High Stakes.** Ava's summons; the Kestrel Tower vault heist —
  lobby security, the terminal, the ledger choice (give / burn / keep), the escape.
- **Chapter 4 — War Path.** Turf war in Old Quarter, grappling training, Lucy
  Fang's scrapyard duel, Tommy's letter.
- **Chapter 5 — Leverage.** The meet and the ambush, the container-yard rescue
  (loud or quiet), the villa finale: kill Vito, take his deal, hand the ledger
  to Brant, or give it all to Kestrel.

**Endings:** BLOOD LEDGER / THE NEW LEDGER / CLEAN STREETS / KESTREL CITY —
each with a written epilogue about what the city becomes.

## 6. Opening cinematic (what the player sees)

Camera flights over the live city: down the Cinder River at dusk, up Main
Cross toward the great bridge, into the Old Quarter market. Rizzo and Dez
converse in person (portraits, typewriter dialogue). Tommy's letter is read.
The UNKNOWN CALLER phones: *"His debt is your debt. Welcome to leverage."*
Dez answers back. Chapter card. Tutorialized entry.

## 7. Tone rules (for all DE YOUNG GAMES writing)

- Short lines. Street cadence. No monologues over six seconds.
- Violence has consequences on screen: bystanders flee, birds scatter, sirens come.
- Nobody is purely evil; everyone is leveraged.
- The city always gets the last word.
